The story so far

Fri 23 Oct, 11:02 · you asked

“Marketing wants to send 15% off codes for Black Friday. Add a coupon field to checkout that validates codes against Stripe and shows the discount before payment.”

What this is. A coupon-code box on the checkout page of the Acme shop. Shoppers type a code, the shop checks it with Stripe (the payment provider), and the new total shows before they pay.

Why it was needed. Marketing is emailing 15%-off codes for Black Friday on Fri 30 Oct. Until now the shop had no place to enter a code, so the campaign could not run.

How it went. The field, the Stripe check and the cart math were built on Fri 23 Oct and Sat 24 Oct. A review on Tue 27 Oct found that expired codes showed a blank error and that totals could round one cent wrong; both are fixed. Testing stopped at payment because staging's Stripe key has expired.

What changed from your ask. You added a limit of one code per order on Mon 26 Oct. Whether codes stack with sale prices never came up until the review, so that is still open.

More of the story · 1 paragraph

How the discount is computed. The discount is applied per line item in cents and rounded down, so the total never shows a fraction of a cent. The cart recomputes on every quantity change, and the code is re-checked with Stripe right before payment in case it expired mid-session.
